How it works

Selling Your Land Can Be Simple

From your first message to a straightforward closing, here's exactly what to expect when you work with Forty Properties.

  1. 01

    Tell Us About Your Property

    Submit the contact form or give us a call. We'll gather some basic information about the property.

  2. 02

    We Review the Property

    We'll look at the property details and determine whether it is a fit for us.

  3. 03

    Receive Your Offer

    If we're interested, we'll present you with a straightforward cash offer.

  4. 04

    Decide What Works for You

    There is no obligation to accept the offer. You decide whether moving forward makes sense for you.

  5. 05

    Close

    If you accept, we'll coordinate the next steps toward closing and work to make the process as smooth as possible.
